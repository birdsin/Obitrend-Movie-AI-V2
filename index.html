Frequently Asked Questions

What is Motion Studio?

Motion Studio transfers movement from a reference video onto a reference image using Kling 3.0 Motion Control.

Which orientation should I choose?

Image keeps the generated character orientation aligned to the source image. Video follows the reference video's character orientation and framing.

How long can the reference video be?

The reference must be at least 3 seconds. Image orientation supports up to 10 seconds; video orientation supports up to 30 seconds.

How are credits calculated?

Motion Control is priced by generated duration: 9 OBITREND credits per second at 720p/Standard and 12 credits per second at 1080p/Professional. The final reservation is calculated server-side before generation.

Can I save the generated video?

Yes. Open or download the result, or save it directly to My Movies / Saved Videos after generation.
